Descriptive & temporal analysis

What is present, and what changes over time?

The indexed corpus is highly concentrated: the three largest publisher sites contribute 82.6% of reviewed claims, so aggregate patterns partly reflect the publisher mix. The EDA establishes baseline patterns in coverage, metadata quality, ratings, claimants, language, and topics, then follows record-level change across calendar periods. Temporal views audit missing dates and publisher-composition changes and remain descriptive rather than causal.

Generative AI and Hoax Distribution

When do strict AI-linked records emerge around ChatGPT's public launch?

In balanced windows, rule-confirmed AI-linked records move from 0 of 1,649 pre-launch claims to 7 of 2,943 post-launch claims. With only seven post-launch records and no pre-launch baseline events, the result supports observed emergence—not a causal generative-AI effect.
