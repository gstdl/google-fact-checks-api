Generative AI and Hoax Distribution

When do strict AI-linked records emerge around ChatGPT's public launch?

In balanced windows, rule-confirmed AI-linked records move from 0 of 1,666 pre-launch (before 30 November 2022) claims to 8 of 2,998 post-launch claims. With only 8 post-launch records and no pre-launch baseline events, the result supports observed emergence—not a causal generative-AI effect.

What this evidence cannot establish

The reports establish what appears in this extract. They do not establish population prevalence, broad transformation, or causation.

Corpus and relationship boundaries

This is a query-conditioned view of Google's indexed fact-check corpus—not a census of Indonesian misinformation. Network relationships are observed associations, not evidence of diffusion, coordination, endorsement, influence, or causality.

Sparse AI-linked evidence

The small rule-confirmed subset documents observed emergence, but it cannot support a claim of broad transformation or estimate the prevalence of AI-linked claims on social media.

Next steps

Planned evidence extensions: referred articles and a separate social-media layer

No article scraper or social-media collector is part of the current evidence. Future work should add these as bounded layers without changing the reviewed-claim grain:

  • Referred-article text: retrieve public fact-check pages referenced by review_url to analyze the claim context, review narrative, cited evidence, and explicitly linked sources.
  • Availability audit: record the check time, HTTP status, redirects, final URL, content type, and content hash. A single failed request means unavailable at check—not taken down; label content confirmed removed only after repeated 404/410 checks plus manual verification or an explicit publisher notice.
  • Responsible retrieval: honor robots directives, site terms, rate limits, access controls, and copyright; do not bypass authentication or paywalls.
  • Separate populations: public posts will remain distinct from fact-checked reviewed claims and retrieved article content.
  • No premature labels: a collected post without a validated fact-check match or human adjudication will be treated as an unreviewed public claim—not labeled a hoax merely because it was collected.
  • Separate denominators: article and social-post counts will not be appended to the fact-check denominator.
  • Decisions before code: platform, access, ethics, sampling, adjudication, retention, and linkage rules must be decided before collectors are introduced.
